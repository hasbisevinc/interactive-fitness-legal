4. Health and safety

4.1 Not medical advice

The app is a game and a general exercise tool. It is not a medical device. It does not provide, and is not a substitute for, medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, physiotherapy or rehabilitation. Nothing in the app is an exercise programme tailored to your health.

4.2 Check with your doctor first

Talk to a doctor before using the app if any of the following apply to you:

  • heart or circulatory disease, high or low blood pressure, or a history of chest pain or palpitations,
  • asthma or another respiratory condition,
  • diabetes or another metabolic condition,
  • joint, knee, hip, back, neck or spine problems, or osteoporosis,
  • recent surgery, a fracture, sprain or other injury,
  • pregnancy or the period after giving birth,
  • balance problems, dizziness or a history of fainting,
  • epilepsy or a history of seizures,
  • a long period of inactivity, older age, or any condition that limits physical activity.

4.3 Photosensitive epilepsy warning

The games contain fast-moving images, bright colours and flashing effects. For example, in Stretch the screen flashes red when you hit a wall. A very small number of people may have a seizure when exposed to such images, even if they have never had one before. If you or a family member has a history of epilepsy or seizures, consult a doctor before playing. Stop immediately and see a doctor if you experience dizziness, altered vision, eye or muscle twitching, loss of awareness, disorientation or involuntary movements.

4.4 While playing

  • Warm up briefly before you start, and cool down and stretch when you finish.
  • Don’t push beyond your limits. If you can’t do a move fully, make it smaller or skip it. Never risk yourself for a score.
  • Stop immediately if you feel chest pain, shortness of breath, dizziness, nausea, unusual fatigue or any pain. If symptoms persist, seek medical help.
  • Drink enough water. Don’t play in very hot, stuffy or humid rooms.
  • Play in non-slip sports shoes or barefoot on a non-slip surface. Don’t play in socks.
  • Don’t play under the influence of alcohol, drugs or medication that affects your attention or balance.
  • Calories, scores and stars are in-game estimates only. Don’t base nutrition or health decisions on them.
  • If you have knee or back problems, you can use “Turn off crouch obstacles” in Settings. It reduces the risk but does not remove it.

5. Play area, phone and TV

  • Free space: keep at least 2–3 metres of free space in front of your phone, and enough width that your outstretched arms touch nothing. Choose a ceiling high enough that you won’t hit your head when jumping.
  • Obstacles: stay away from furniture, glass, windows, stairs, sharp corners and stoves or heaters. Rug edges, cables and objects on the floor can trip you.
  • Others: keep other people, especially small children, and pets out of the play area.
  • Floor: don’t play on wet, slippery or uneven floors.
  • Phone: place your phone securely on a stable surface or stand where it can’t fall or tip over and where you won’t hit it. Don’t hold it while playing. The phone may get warm during long sessions; take a break if it overheats.
  • TV: make sure your TV is secured and its cables are out of the play area. Keep a safe distance from the TV and screen.
  • Neighbours and home: jumping can be noisy and puts load on the floor. Respect building rules and your neighbours.

You are responsible for the safety of your play area, your devices and the objects around you.

6. Estimates and motion detection

  • Your movements are detected on the device from the camera image. Lighting, clothing, camera angle, distance, device performance and framing can delay detection, make it inaccurate, or cause a move to be missed. Game results, scores and passed or failed obstacles may be affected.
  • Calorie values are rough estimates based only on play time and movement counts, without personal data such as age, weight or heart rate. They do not reflect your actual energy expenditure.
  • We do not guarantee that the app works on every device, network, AirPlay or Chromecast device or TV. TV mirroring depends on your network, devices and third-party software.
