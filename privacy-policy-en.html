1. Scope

This policy covers the Interactive Fitness app for iPhone, including its “City Run” and “Stretch” games, and its TV mirroring features. The app has no accounts, ads or in-app purchases. It does not request or use your location, contacts, photo library, Apple Health (HealthKit) data or advertising identifier (IDFA).

2. Camera and motion detection

  • The games find your body’s joints (shoulders, elbows, hips, knees, etc.) in the front camera image using Apple’s on-device Vision technology. From these, jumps, crouches, lane changes and limb angles are calculated.
  • Images and movement data are processed only in the moment, in your device’s memory. No image, video, photo or body measurement is stored or leaves your device, whether to us, to Google or to anyone else.
  • This is not used to recognise or identify you. There is no face recognition and no biometric template is created, so no special-category (biometric) data is processed.
  • When you mirror the game to a TV, what is sent to the TV is the game’s picture, not your camera image (see section 5).
  • TV delay measurement: if you start it (or accept it when the app offers it because the TV looks late), you point the phone’s rear camera at the TV. The app only looks for when a marker shown in the game appears on the TV screen, to measure the delay. This image, too, is processed only in memory at that moment; it is not stored or sent anywhere.
  • iOS asks for camera permission. You can turn it off at any time in the iPhone Settings app; the games then can’t detect your movements.

3. Data stored on your device

The following is stored only on your iPhone, in the app’s own storage, and is never sent to us:

  • Run history: for each finished game, the date and time, which game, score, stars, estimated calories, duration, obstacle/wall counts, number of jumps, crouches and lane changes, and whether it was played on the phone or a TV.
  • Settings and preferences: music and sound effects, the crouch-obstacle preference, reminder on/off and time, the game last shown on the home screen, figure visibility in Stretch, in-game settings.
  • TV settings: for each TV you used the game on with settings of its own (Settings ▸ TV settings): the TV’s name (the AirPlay receiver’s or Chromecast’s name), where the game’s sound plays (TV, phone or off), the resolution and graphics choices, the last measured delay and when it was measured. The app also remembers the names of TVs for which it has already offered the delay measurement, so it does not offer it again.
  • Consent records: that the introduction was shown, the version of the health notice you accepted, and your answer to the usage-data question.

Calorie values are rough estimates based only on play time and movement counts. They are not medical or health data.

If you use iCloud or computer backups, this data may be included in your device backup under Apple’s privacy policy. You can delete all of it with Settings ▸ Erase my data or by deleting the app.

4. Usage statistics and crash reports (only with your consent)

The app asks you the first time you open it. If you say no, no statistics or crash reports are sent, and every feature still works. You can change your mind at any time in Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ “Usage and crash data”. When you withdraw consent, sending stops and the analytics identifier and any unsent reports on your device are deleted.

If you consent, Google’s Firebase services are used:

Google Analytics for Firebase

  • Usage events: screens opened; games started, paused and finished; score, stars, estimated calories, duration and movement counts; phone or TV play; TV search and connection results; TV delay measurement (started, offered by the app, the delays measured in each step, the result and the settings applied) and TV settings changes (sound, resolution, graphics), without the TV’s name; stream performance numbers on a TV (picture and sound delay, encoding time, dropped frames, freezes); camera, local network and notification permission results; settings changes (sound, music, crouch obstacles, reminder and its time); when a feature suggestion is sent, only its length (never its text); data erasure.
  • Technical data: a randomly generated app instance ID, device model, iOS version, app version, language, screen resolution, first-open and session times. Google may derive an approximate location (country/city) from your IP address; Google Analytics does not store IP addresses.
  • The advertising identifier (IDFA) is not collected, there is no cross-app tracking, and the data is not used for advertising. The analytics ID is not linked to your name or email.

Firebase Crashlytics

  • If the app crashes or hits a serious error: technical details of the error (stack trace), device model, iOS and app version, memory and disk state, orientation, time of the crash, and a random Crashlytics installation ID.
  • A short log of the app events just before the crash (the usage events above), where the game was being played, and the crouch-obstacle preference.

5. Playing on a TV

  • Local network: to find your TV, the app looks for AirPlay and Chromecast devices on your Wi‑Fi (Bonjour and Google Cast discovery). iOS asks for “Local Network” permission for this. Found device names are shown on your screen and are never sent. If a TV gets settings of its own (section 3), its name is stored on your device with them.
  • AirPlay: mirroring uses Apple’s iOS feature and is subject to Apple’s privacy policy. The app sends none of your data during it.
  • Chromecast: the game’s picture and sound are streamed from your phone to your TV only within your own local network, over an encrypted (WebRTC) connection. They are not sent over the internet or to us. Your camera image is never streamed. To measure the delay, the receiver page on your Chromecast sends stream statistics (such as delay and dropped frames) back to your phone over the same local connection.
  • Google Cast SDK: Chromecast support uses Google’s Cast software kit, which starts only when you search for a TV or connect to a Chromecast. Once started, Google may collect diagnostic and performance data, a device identifier, approximate location and Cast usage data to monitor and improve the reliability of the service. Your Chromecast loads the receiver app through Google’s Cast infrastructure. Google LLC processes this data under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • The app never uses your microphone. iOS requires the microphone permission text only because of the WebRTC library used for streaming. When the game’s sound is set to play on the phone while the picture is on a TV, iOS’s “play and record” audio mode is used only to keep the sound on the phone; the microphone is not opened.

6. Feature suggestion form

You can choose to write and send a message from Settings ▸ “Suggest a feature”. Only the text you write is sent (up to 1,000 characters). Your name, email or device identifiers are not added. The message is stored on servers run on Supabase Inc.’s infrastructure, which may be outside Türkiye. Your IP address may be kept briefly in Supabase’s security and operational logs. Please don’t include personal information (name, phone, email, health details, etc.). If you do, we use it only to consider your suggestion and delete it on request.

7. Notifications and other permissions

  • Reminder notifications: the daily workout reminder is scheduled locally on your iPhone. No notification server is used and no push token is collected. Notification permission is asked only if you want reminders. You can turn the reminder off or change its time in Settings.
  • The app does not use location, the microphone or other sensors in the background.
  • The analytics and cloud services of Unity, the game engine the app uses, are turned off.

10. Retention

  • Camera image and movement data: not stored; held in memory only while being processed.
  • Data on your device: until you use “Erase my data” or delete the app.
  • Firebase Analytics: for the period set in the Google Analytics data-retention setting, at most 14 months, then deleted automatically. Aggregated, non-identifying reports may be kept longer.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports for up to 90 days.
  • Suggestion messages: deleted within one year after they have been considered.
  • Data-protection requests and correspondence: for the general limitation period after the request is closed.

11. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data. Data that leaves the device (Firebase, Supabase) is sent over encrypted HTTPS. The Chromecast stream uses an encrypted WebRTC connection on your local network. On-device data is kept in the app’s iOS sandbox. Access is limited to the developer. If a data breach occurs, affected users and the competent authority (the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board and, where applicable, an EU/UK authority) will be notified as soon as possible.

12. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Children should only use it under adult supervision. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Consent to usage data must be given by a parent or legal guardian on a child’s behalf. If you believe a child’s data has been processed, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Your rights

Under KVKK art. 11 you may ask us whether your personal data is processed and request information about it; learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly; know the third parties it is transferred to in Türkiye or abroad; have it corrected if incomplete or inaccurate; have it deleted or destroyed; have third parties notified of such corrections or deletions; object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis; and claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing. You may withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing before the withdrawal (Settings ▸ Privacy).

Note: because the app has no accounts and the data is not linked to your name, we usually cannot find the anonymous data sent to Firebase as yours. The fastest way to remove it is to switch consent off in the app and use “Erase my data”, which resets the analytics identifier so earlier data can no longer be linked to you.

Send requests to flashasbi@gmail.com.
